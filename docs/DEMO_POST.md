Cross-chain quotes are hard to compare, and a 1Click deposit address is easy to fund by mistake.

Instinct Router asks NEAR Intents 1Click for a dry quote (`dry: true`), ranks it against Relay on minimum output, fee, and ETA, and leaves sending off. A stored run at 2026-11-14T18:43:50.764Z ranked 20 pairs: Relay won 17, 1Click won 3. Fifteen of the Relay wins were a shorter ETA inside a 0.50% output band. The two NEAR USDC destinations had no Relay quote. The quote hash from the demo pair is on NEAR testnet contract `ir1cd33a844b.testnet`. No wallet is connected and no funds move.
